What are the problems in construction?

The usual ones are delays, budget overruns and unclear plans. We agree the scope, the schedule and a fixed price before work starts, and report progress every week.

What is best practice in construction?

Clear documentation, licensed crews, regular site inspections and transparent communication between client, architect and contractor at every milestone.

How long does a typical project take?

Small renovations take about 2 weeks, full home builds 8 weeks or more. Every package starts with a fixed timeline agreed before work begins.
